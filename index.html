Our Love Story

Our journey began in a way neither of us could have planned. It came through my mother, whose love is rooted in prayer and whose heart always hopes for God's best. From the very start, my desire was simple: that you would feel respected, safe, and at peace, always knowing your heart mattered to me.
I knew it was love when being with you felt like rest. You softened places I had guarded for a long time. With you, laughter feels lighter, silence feels safe, and even difficult days feel possible. You became my peace in prayer and my joy in the ordinary moments that matter most.
Today, I do not only love you. I choose you. I choose to walk with you under God's guidance, to grow with you, and to build a life where faith and love remain at the center. This is our story — it began softly, guided by prayer, and today it becomes a sacred promise.

A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.

I choose you, Clara Nana Mensah, before God and before love, now and always.
